# Oceanalt

Oceanalt is run as a small firm of Claude agents plus the founder. Since 17 Nov 2026 (founder's call) it is a **design and technology studio for established Australian brands**: brand identity, websites, web apps and software, AI agents, voice agents and custom audio, payments, search and AI visibility, and care after launch. Every project is quoted in writing after a first call. **No prices anywhere on the site**, not even "from". **The website is free for brands that take payments through our gateway**; we take a platform fee per payment, with the rate in the written agreement only, never on the site (BUSINESS_PLAN section 0). The old small-business plans (Care, Launch, Grow, one-off, AI concierge, chat assistant) are retired for new clients; existing clients keep theirs (BUSINESS_PLAN section 0). Client sites and apps are hosted on Cloudflare and AWS, and client data stays in Australia (AWS Sydney); keep that true (see OPERATIONS.md).

**The site** (Sydney and Melbourne; founder: "something like hypragency.com"; study in `docs/design/STUDIO_STUDY.md`): white, ink `#0d1114`, soft grey `#f3f4f4`, one deep teal `#1f3a46` for the footer and dark panels, Geist at weight 500, a big uppercase centred statement, pill buttons with the arrow in its own circle, lots of space, slow eased motion that stops for reduced motion. The homepage is `src/studio/Studio.tsx` (copy in `src/studio/content.ts`, styles in `src/studio/studio.css`): statement, a strip of sample work, tools, swipeable services, "AI agents for your business" (an example agent log for Corella Hotels and a real-looking phone that rings: pick up, or decline and the agent answers anyway, to hear a guest's late check-in handled by "Corella's AI agent"; `src/studio/CallPhone.tsx`, lines and timings in `src/data/sample-call-hotel.json`, audio `public/audio/concierge-hotel.mp3`, ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, Kate as the agent and Jack as the guest), "Automate the way your business runs" (one Calloway Day Surgery admission moving through Gmail, Calendar, Stripe and Xero; service page `/workflow-automation/`), "why one studio", the free website and payments, the process loop, a dark work band, a note from Lathif, FAQ, the enquiry form, teal footer. `/design/` (`src/seo/DesignPage.tsx`), `/designs/` and the service pages (`src/seo/landing.ts`) are static and prerendered with the same look (`src/seo/StaticChrome.tsx`, `src/seo/static.css`). Old small-business URLs redirect in `public/_redirects`. Photos live in `public/photos/` with credits in `CREDITS.md`.

**Write like a good Australian studio:** plain, direct, a bit dry, Australian spelling, short sentences, no hype or American sales talk. No invented stats, testimonials, client logos, awards or scarcity ("limited spots" and "trusted by 100+" are exactly what we don't do). Sample designs are always labelled as fictional. Never promise rankings.

## The 15-minute reply (`api/start.ts`)
A Vercel function: the start form posts to it, Claude recommends a plan and layout as structured output, and Resend emails the reply to the enquirer and the lead to us. It falls back to the Firebase/EmailJS path if its env vars aren't set. Internally Oceanalt runs on AI agents, checked by people, but on the site we don't say the sites are built by AI (founder's call, 12 Oct 2026): lead with the founder, Lathif Shaik, who designs, signs off every site and answers the phone (his card by the start form, his name in the footer; his photo is `public/team/lathif.webp` via `FOUNDER_PHOTO` in `src/App.tsx`, and he is titled "Builder"). Don't mention AI in the trust, start-form or footer copy (founder's call). Keep it true: a person reads every enquiry the same business day. The site only promises the 15-minute reply once this function runs on Cloudflare (where the site is hosted) and passes an end-to-end test; until then it promises a same-business-day reply from a person.

## Retired: selling the AI concierge (CEO decisions, 8 Oct 2026)
The per-call pricing below is for existing clients only; the site shows no prices since 17 Nov 2026. The rules on how agents introduce themselves, the recording notice and "never miss a call" still apply to every voice agent.
- The product is the **AI concierge** on the site. On calls it introduces itself as **"[owner]'s AI agent"**, never a human name, and every call opens with the AI and recording notice (can't be switched off). Never "receptionist" in our copy; "AI receptionist" only in the landing meta description, because buyers search it.
- Never "never miss a call" or any promise that every call is answered. Urgent transfer is "can … if you want".
- Show the cost per call (15 to 20c/min estimate, a 3-minute call about 45 to 60 cents) and put "3-month minimum. Nothing is charged until it passes our test calls" next to the price.
