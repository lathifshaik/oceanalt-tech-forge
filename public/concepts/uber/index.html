How it picks

Every suggestion says why it was picked.

  1. Your usualsEach dish is scored on heat, crunch, richness, freshness, sweetness, tang and depth. Your usuals give us a taste profile.
  2. Somewhere newPlaces and cuisines you haven't ordered from rank higher. The slider sets how far from your usual it goes.
  3. Nearby and openCloser places and shorter waits win ties. Closed kitchens don't show.
  4. Order on Uber EatsEach pick opens in Uber Eats, where you order and pay as usual.
