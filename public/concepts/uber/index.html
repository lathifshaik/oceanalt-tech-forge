How it picks.

No black box. Every suggestion shows why it was picked, and you can push it closer to your usual or further away.

  • Your taste

    Each dish is scored on heat, crunch, richness, freshness, sweetness, tang and depth. Your usuals make a taste profile.

  • Something new

    Places and cuisines you haven't ordered from rank higher. The slider sets how far from your usual it goes.

  • Close and open

    Nearer places and shorter waits win ties. Closed kitchens never show.

  • Always says why

    Every pick shows the flavours it shares with your usual and what's new about it.
