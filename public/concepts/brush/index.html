01  Brush 01 · Design concept

A clear electric toothbrush with a gap camera and a rinse jet. Every part that does the work is in plain sight.

02  Shot 1 of 4

Made to get wet.

Sealed clear shell. Drop it in, rinse it off, leave it by the sink.

03  Details

Most brushes hide everything in white plastic. Brush 01 shows the battery, the board, the motor and the water, sealed in one clear shell.

Timer

Four light bars glow through the handle. One goes out every 30 seconds.

Rinse tank

A clear cell at the top of the handle. You can see how much is left.

Charge ring

The dock shows the battery as a ring of 36 dots. No port on the brush.

Controls

One button. Press to start, hold to change mode.

04  Specs

Design specs for the concept. Brush 01 isn't a product you can buy.

Clear, sealed
Motor
