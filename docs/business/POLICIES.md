# Oceanalt policies

_Written 20 November 2026 for the studio (BUSINESS_PLAN section 0). Owner: `ceo` agent + founder. Every agent works to these. If a policy here and the Terms on the site ever disagree, the Terms win until both are fixed the same day._

How a design and technology studio for established Australian brands should run, written so each rule also shows clients why we're a safer pair of hands than a big agency or a freelancer. Plain words, no prices. Prices live in each written proposal, never on the site or in outreach.

## What these policies protect: our advantages

Every policy below exists to keep one of these true. If a rule ever works against one of them, change the rule.

1. **One studio, one team.** The person who designs it builds it. Brand, site, software, AI agents, voice and payments in one place, so nothing falls between two agencies.
2. **You deal with the founder.** Lathif runs every project, signs off every page and answers the phone. No account managers.
3. **Fixed, written quotes.** Scope and price agreed in writing before work starts. Nothing billed that wasn't approved first.
4. **The website can be free.** Brands that take payments through our gateway get the site designed, built and hosted at no charge; we earn a small platform fee per payment instead. We only do well when they do.
5. **You own everything.** Domain in the client's name from day one; code and designs theirs once paid. No lock-in, no hostage fees.
6. **Data stays in Australia.** Client sites on Cloudflare, customer data in AWS Sydney. Any overseas service is named in writing.
7. **Fast, because the tooling is ours.** Our own design system, builder and agents mean stages that take agencies weeks take us days. People check everything.
8. **Honest AI.** Our agents always say they're AI, every call says it's recorded, and a person approves anything that matters.

## 1. Who we work with

- **Yes:** established Australian brands that care about craft, roughly 20 to 500 staff. Property, hospitality groups, health groups, professional firms, retail and consumer brands.
- **Say no (politely, same day):** work we can't do well, deadlines we can't meet honestly, gambling, adult, weapons, payday lending, political campaigns, anything misleading or unlawful, and briefs that ask us to copy another company's site or brand.
- **Existing small-business clients** keep their plans and terms (section 18). We don't take new small-business plans.
- **Go/no-go on large or unusual work** (over 3 months, or anything regulated such as health records or financial advice tools): `ceo` recommends, founder decides, before a proposal goes out.

## 2. Enquiries and the first call

- A person reads every enquiry the same business day (Sydney time) and replies to set up a call. The site doesn't promise a 15-minute reply until `api/start.ts` runs on Cloudflare and passes an end-to-end test.
- The first call is free, about 30 minutes, with Lathif. We listen first: what the brand does, what's not working, what a good result looks like, dates, who decides.
- No pitch decks, no "discovery fee", no pressure. If we're not the right fit we say so and, where we can, name who might be.
- After the call, the proposal follows within **3 business days** for most projects, or we say when it'll come.

## 3. Proposals and quotes

Every proposal is written, in plain English, and includes:

- **Scope:** what we'll make, page by page or feature by feature, and what's not included.
- **Price:** a fixed price per stage. No hourly billing on projects.
- **Timeline:** set around their launch date, split into stages so they see working pieces early.
- **Payment schedule** (section 4), **running costs** passed on at cost (domains, software, AI usage, call time) with a monthly cap, and **care after launch** as a separate, optional line.
- **Assumptions:** what we need from them and by when (content, access, feedback). If those slip, the date moves, not the quality.
- **Which providers we'll use** and where data is stored (section 10).
- Valid for 30 days.

Never in a proposal: invented results, ranking promises, or "starting from" teasers.

## 4. Paying for projects

- **Deposit before work starts**, then milestones tied to things the client can see (for example design approved, site on a preview link, launch). Recommended default: 40% deposit, 40% at preview, 20% at launch; larger builds by stage.
- Invoices from Stripe, due within **14 days**, showing the ABN and "No GST" until we're registered (register before turnover heads past A$75,000 a year, and update prices and invoices that week).
- **Late payment:** a reminder at day 3 overdue, a call at day 10. Work pauses (with written notice) if a milestone is over 30 days late. We don't take a live site down over a dispute without the notice the Terms set out.
- We never ask for full payment up front, and nothing launches until the client has approved it.

## 5. The free website with payments

The headline offer, so it has to be clear and fair.

- **Who qualifies:** brands that take real payments online (bookings, deposits, orders, memberships, tickets, invoices) and agree to process them through our gateway. Brands that don't take payments get the site quoted as a project.
- **What's free:** strategy, design, build and hosting of the website described in the agreement, plus keeping it secure and up to date. Larger extras (apps, integrations, AI agents, brand work) are quoted as usual.
- **How we earn:** a platform fee on each payment through the gateway, on top of the payment provider's processing fee. **The rate is in the written agreement only**, never on the site, in outreach or in a public post. No sales, no fee.
- **Where the money goes:** straight into the client's own account (Stripe Connect). We never hold client funds or card data. Every payment and fee shows in their own dashboard.
- **Minimum term and leaving:** set in the agreement before work starts, and explained on the first call, not in fine print. See the recommended defaults in section 19.
- **Fair use:** if a brand's online payments turn out far lower than expected, we talk about it; we don't quietly change the deal.

## 6. Changes and approvals

- Each stage has **two rounds of changes** included. Feedback goes in one list from one person on their side.
- Anything new (a page, feature, integration or a change of direction after approval) is a **change request**: we write down what it is, what it costs and what it does to the date, and start only once they've said yes in writing. Never do it and bill later.
- Nothing goes live without the client's written approval.

## 7. Care after launch

Offered with every project, never compulsory. Agreed in writing with:

- Hosting, security updates, backups and uptime monitoring.
- A set amount of changes each month, done by the people who built it, and a short monthly report in plain English with what we did and one or two things worth improving.
- Notice period of 30 days to end it, and a proper handover (code, access, documentation) when they leave.

## 8. Support and response times

Kept modest so we can always beat it. During business hours (9am to 5pm Sydney, Monday to Friday, except public holidays):

| What | We respond within | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Site or payments down | 2 business hours | Lathif's phone first; fix or a clear workaround the same day where we can |
| Something broken but working | 1 business day | Fixed in the next 2 business days, or a date given |
| A change request | 1 business day | Done within the care allowance, or quoted first |

Uptime monitoring alerts us out of hours; we act on outages as soon as we can but don't promise 24/7 cover unless a care agreement says so and is priced for it.

## 9. Ownership

- The client owns their content, brand assets and data from the start. **Domains are registered in their name** from day one.
- Designs and code we make for them are theirs once the final invoice is paid. Our general tools and know-how stay ours; they get a licence to use any of it included in their work.
- Fonts, photos, voices and software are licensed properly and, where needed, in the client's name. No unlicensed assets, ever.
- We only show client work in our portfolio with their written OK. Until then the portfolio uses fictional samples, labelled as such.

## 10. Data, privacy and hosting

- Sites on Cloudflare; client apps, databases, files, forms and bookings in **AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2)**, backups included.
- Every overseas processor (some AI and telephony providers, for example) is named in the proposal and the client's privacy wording, and we only use settings that don't train on client data.
- We follow the Australian Privacy Principles, collect only what a feature needs, and delete client data on request at the end of an engagement.
- **A data breach:** contain it, tell the client within 24 hours of knowing, help them assess it under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, and write up what changed.
- Access is per person, with two-factor authentication on every account that touches client data. Agents get the least access a job needs.

## 11. AI agents, voice and audio

- Every agent says it's an AI agent. **Every phone call opens by saying so and that the call is recorded**, and that can't be switched off. On calls it's "[brand]'s AI agent", never a human name.
- The client signs off what the agent may do on its own and what goes to a person. Anything involving money above a set amount, complaints, health, legal or safety goes to a person. No advice outside the brief; emergencies get "call triple zero".
- Running costs (AI usage, call minutes, numbers) at cost with a monthly cap; at the cap, calls fall back to voicemail or a person.
- Ten test calls pass before any agent goes live (`CONCIERGE_SETUP.md`), plus a lawyer's check of the greeting against the recording laws in each state the agent works in.
- **Voices:** licensed library voices, or a custom voice designed for the brand. We never clone a real person's voice without their written consent, and never imitate a public figure.
- We never say "never miss a call" or promise every call is answered.

## 12. Quality and accessibility

- Every launch passes the QA checklist in `OPERATIONS.md`: phone and desktop, speed, forms, payments in test mode, redirects, no console errors.
- We design to **WCAG 2.2 AA**. We tell the client where something they've asked for would fall short.
- Real photography or properly licensed images only. Every design is unique; the builder refuses one another client already has.

## 13. Honesty in marketing (ours and theirs)

- No invented stats, testimonials, reviews, client logos, awards, case studies or scarcity. Not on our site, not on sites we build.
- Sample designs are always labelled as fictional.
- Never promise rankings, AI mentions or results. We describe what we'll do, not what Google will do.
- We won't write fake reviews for clients or build anything designed to mislead their customers.

## 14. Outreach and follow-up

Two tiers, both under the Spam Act 2003 (full rules and templates in `docs/growth/OUTREACH.md`):

- **Cold** (we found them; they haven't spoken to us): one message and one follow-up per channel, at most one phone call, then stop. Only to addresses the business publishes, every message says who we are with our ABN and a way to opt out, and opt-outs are honoured the same day.
- **Warm** (they enquired, met us, or asked us to follow up): up to **six to eight touches** over a few weeks, each with something useful (an answer, an idea, a draft), never "just checking in" twice in a row.
- **When to stop:** they say no or stop, it's clearly not a fit, or a warm lead goes quiet after the last touch. Note the reason in the Notion Sales pipeline, and only try again in 3 months if they said "not now".
- The founder sends every message. Agents draft.

## 15. Pausing, ending and refunds

- A client can end a project by written notice. They pay for work done to that point and get everything they've paid for.
- Deposits cover work already booked; if we end a project for reasons on our side, we refund what isn't covered by delivered work.
- Nothing here limits rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If something we made doesn't work as promised, we fix it at our cost.

## 16. Confidentiality

- Client information stays confidential and is used only for their project. We sign a reasonable NDA when asked.
- Agents never put client data into tools that train on it, and never share one client's details with another.

## 17. Complaints

- Any complaint goes to Lathif, who acknowledges it within 1 business day and proposes a fix within 5.
- We write down what happened and what changed so it doesn't happen twice.
- If we can't agree, we suggest mediation before anything else.

## 18. Existing small-business clients

- Clients on Care, Launch, Grow, the AI concierge or chat assistant keep their plan, price, edit allowance and terms (BUSINESS_PLAN sections 3 and 3c). Edits: Care $60 each; Launch 30 minutes a month; Grow 1 hour a month; no rollover; bigger work quoted first.
- The 27 businesses quoted the 1 November outreach offer get that price if they reply.
- If they ask about the studio's services, quote as normal; never pressure them to move.

## 19. Open decisions, with recommended defaults

The founder decides these; `ceo` recommends. They go in the agreement template, never on the site. Get the lawyer to review before the first signing.

| Decision | Recommended default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee rate | Set per client from expected online revenue, in a band of **1% to 2%** of each payment, plus the provider's processing fee | Low enough that an established brand doing real volume sees it as fair next to a project fee; high enough that a two-year term repays the build |
| Minimum term | **24 months** on the free website | Covers the build at modest volumes; long enough to matter, short enough to sign |
| Low-volume floor | A monthly minimum fee in the agreement if gateway fees fall under an agreed level for three months running | Protects us from a free site with no sales, without changing the deal quietly |
| Leaving early or moving off the gateway | They **keep the site** and pay the remaining build value: the build value stated in the agreement, reduced evenly each month of the term | Fair, easy to explain, no hostage website; avoids ACL issues with taking sites down |
| After the term | Stay on the gateway with no minimum, or move to a normal care agreement | They choose; nothing auto-renews into a lock-in |
| Project deposit | 40% / 40% / 20% (section 4) | Standard for studio work; keeps cash ahead of time spent |
| Stripe Connect | Apply as a platform now; a payment provider review can take weeks | Nothing on the free-website offer can be signed until it's approved |
