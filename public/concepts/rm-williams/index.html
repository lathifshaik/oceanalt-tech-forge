One piece: the story of a boot cut from a single piece of leather. A concept by Oceanalt.

South Australia, 1932

A bushman sat down to make a boot.He had a piece of leather, a knife and a long way to walk.

He didn't want parts.Parts come apart. He wanted one piece.

Scroll

One hide.Move the light across it. Every mark is the animal's own.

Nothing stitched on.No toe cap, no side panels. Just the hide.

Cut it whole.The pattern goes on the hide in one line. The knife follows it all the way round.

Then fold it round the foot.From one piece. That's the whole trick.

One seam.At the back, where it meets itself.

Sewn by hand.In a workshop in Adelaide. Still.

Then it went walking.Stock routes, red dirt, a lot of creek crossings.

It never stopped.Same boot. Different streets.

One piece.
Since 1932.
